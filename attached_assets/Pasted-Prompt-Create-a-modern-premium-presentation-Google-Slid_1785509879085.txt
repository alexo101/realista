Important Product Narrative

Throughout the presentation emphasize:

Realista is horizontal.

It manages the complete agency workflow.

AppPro is vertical.

It specializes in home services.

The vision is not to replace AppPro.

The vision is to embed AppPro as the Home Services module inside Realista.

This allows Papernest to move from owning only the moving moment to owning the complete real estate customer journey.
